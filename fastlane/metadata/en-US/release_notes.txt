A little less clutter.

• Clean up conversations across your server by age or project, in a dedicated sheet.
• Review chats individually: swipe right to keep, left to queue for deletion, and undo a choice before confirming.
• Open read-only transcripts before deciding what to delete. Running chats and conversations with active child agents are excluded from cleanup.
• Follow live subagent activity and open agent conversations from the new agent sheet.
• Enjoy responsive native chat navigation on iPhone and iPad.
• Tables keep their columns aligned after replies finish, with sideways scrolling for wide tables.

OpenClient supports OpenCode v1 and v2.
